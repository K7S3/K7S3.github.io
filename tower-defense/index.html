Controls

  • Tap / Click a hex plot to build, a tower to inspect, empty space to deselect.
  • Space pause / resume. 1 / 2 / 3 game speed. Esc close panel or pause.
  • Hover a tower on desktop to preview its range.
  • Calling a wave early grants bonus gold. Clearing waves grants more.
  • Selling a tower refunds part of what you spent. Plan ahead.
