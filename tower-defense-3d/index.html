How to Play

Goal

Year 2100. Humanity endures inside sealed arcologies, and the rogue machine swarm called the RUST is coming for them. You are a Bastion Commander defending the COMMAND SPIRE, the heart of the colony and anchor of its tactical uplink network.

The Rust marches on the Spire from the gates. If machines reach it, the Spire takes damage. If it falls, the sector goes dark. Clear every wave to secure the sector and earn up to 3 stars. Every wave you hold keeps the colonists' morale alive.

Controls

  • Build palette: pick a tower, then click a free cell inside the uplink to place it. Right-click or Esc cancels. The mode stays on so you can place several in a row.
  • Wall tool: drag across the ground to draw a wall line. Release to build. Walls block the Rust and buy time.
  • Reactor: raises your energy capacity. Hungry towers go silent without power.
  • Click a structure: inspect, upgrade, specialize (branch A / B at tier 3), repair walls, or sell.
  • Drag empty ground: pan the camera. Wheel: zoom. Right-drag or Q/E: rotate.
  • Space: pause. 1 / 2 / 3: game speed. Esc: cancel placement / close panel.
  • Start Wave sends the next wave early for bonus gold. Call Early rushes the remaining enemies of the active wave.

Command Spire

The Spire projects the tactical uplink: structures outside its radius go DARK and dormant. Upgrade the Spire to push the light further. OVERCLOCK widens the uplink and boosts damage while strain climbs; at 100% the Spire burns itself.

Morale

Colony morale shifts with every wave: inspired, steady, despair, unrest. At unrest, construction freezes until order returns.

Towers

Synergies

Certain towers empower each other when built near one another.

Blackout Surges

Watch the wave preview for surges. A BLACKOUT SURGE INCOMING warning means the uplink is about to shrink. Weather it for salvage bonuses.

Select Sector

Clear a sector to unlock the next. Keep the Spire untouched for 3 stars.
