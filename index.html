About Me

🚀 Hi, I'm Keshavan — a builder, researcher, and entrepreneur at heart.

As a Senior Machine Learning Engineer at Prudential Financial, I built a real-time Table Augmented Generation system on AWS, DuckDB, FastAPI, and GPT-4 — powering a chatbot used by thousands of people. Today I'm at Meta.

I'm the founder of Synergii, an AI-powered grant discovery platform I took through Cornell's Johnson Summer Startup Accelerator. Before that, I researched GPCR activation mechanisms and drug design at IIIT Hyderabad, blending molecular dynamics simulations with machine learning.
